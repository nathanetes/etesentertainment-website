History

How it started

Nathan Etes started making music after watching Billie Eilish's documentary, The World's a Little Blurry. His first tracks were GarageBand loops on an iPad. He saved for a MacBook by doing jobs for neighbors and putting away gift money, then taught himself Logic Pro.

He wrote, produced, and recorded his first EP, CTY-S, over one summer at his family's lake house in northern Wisconsin. It came out in December 2022. Singles followed, including "Keys to My Heart" in 2023.

The business started after a music production and industry leadership program at UCLA. He spent a week there in the Sproul Hall dorms, working in a group to write, produce, record, and mix a song. He produced his single "MOVED ON FIRST" on the flight to Los Angeles and recorded its vocals in the dorm. He formed Etes Entertainment LLC in 2024, with A1 Records as its label.

The company handles each step of putting out his work: production, mixing and mastering, distribution, and registering the catalog with BMI, The MLC, and SoundExchange. His debut album, juniper, came out in 2025, and he has studied audio through Berklee Online since then. All music, lyrics, and art released by the company are human-made.
